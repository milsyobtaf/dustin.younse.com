# About me

## Professional life

Howdy! My name is Dustin. I'm a Senior Design Technologist and Lead Documentation Engineer working on the Design System at [Indeed](https://indeed.com) where we like to say - We help people get jobs! Prior to moving into design systems full time, I was a Drupalist working on Very Large Websites™ for media brands and universities you've definitely heard of. Drupalists were using design systems before they were cool - we just called them [Style Tiles](https://styletil.es).

Before realizing HTML and CSS could pay the bills, I was an aspiring filmmaker who enjoyed the more technical aspects of audio / video editing and visual effects. I was lucky enough to work with amazingly talented folks like the [Duplass Brothers](https://duplassbrothers.com), and had a truly great time building suspense with music and gore on small budget horror films.

## The other 128 hours of the week

I'm a lifelong cyclist who has recently rediscovered riding on dirt. I've got a [dentist quality mountain bike](https://yeticycles.com) and a [gravel bike](https://ninerbikes.com) that isn't quite as fancy, but they both make me feel like I have wings.

I used to have a pretty stacked [garage gym](https://fringesport.com) where my friends and I lifted [heavy things](https://roguefitness.com) and put them back down again. Mostly powerlifting with some strongman aspirations and a dash of HIIT on the weekends to work the beach muscles. After moving back to the Boston area, I look forward to rebuilding the iron paradise.

I like to tinker with [Arduino adjacent](https://adafruit.com) electronics and [3d printers](https://prusa3d.com). When public gatherings were more common, I helped run [dorkbot Austin](https://dorkbot.org) for several years and had a part in getting the nerd party [SXSW Create](https://hackaday.com/2015/03/17/sxsw-create-atx-hackerspace-area/) off the ground.

## On the road again

I've gone back and forth a few times, but now I'm back in Boston, for good. I'll always miss my home state of Texas, and eating all of the tacos I can get my hands on. New England is home now, and I'm eating all of the seafood and meatball subs I can get my hands on. One of these days, I'm going to buy some land in Vermont or New Mexico with my closest friends and enjoy a pastoral lifestyle with high speed internet.

New England has the best bread based foods, but the Southwest has the best tortilla based foods.
