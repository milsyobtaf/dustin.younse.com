One of the most influential hardware products to come out of New England was the PDP line of [minicomputers][minicomp]. These machines were one of the first steps away from the mainframes that ruled the early days of computing. With minicomputers, the hardware was "cheap" enough that an academic or corporate department could buy one for their own use, rather than sharing a machine that served an entire university or corporate campus.
